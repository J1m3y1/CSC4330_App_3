About Fantasi

Curated for individuals who know what they want.

Fantasi was built on a simple premise: that connection and generosity aren't mutually exclusive. We exist for members who know what they want and aren't interested in pretending otherwise. Every profile is verified, every introduction curated, and every arrangement left entirely in the hands of the two people who make it.

Designed for discretion

Some things are better kept between you.

From the first introduction to every connection that follows, Fantasi is designed with privacy at its core. Every interaction is approached with care, giving you the freedom to explore new connections without unnecessary exposure or attention.

Verified, always

Government ID and photo verification for every member, with optional income and background checks for added assurance.

Anonymous by default

No photo, name, or exact location is shown until you choose to share it. Billing never mentions Fantasi by name.

Secured by design

Reviewed on an ongoing basis by cybersecurity professionals. Every piece of sensitive information you enter is encrypted end to end, in transit and at rest.
