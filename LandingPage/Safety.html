01 — How admission works

Three checks, before anyone sees you — or you see them.

Membership is never granted on payment alone. Every profile, free or paid, passes through the same review before it becomes visible to anyone else.

01

Identity verification

Government ID is required before a profile can browse at all. No exceptions, no shortcuts — this applies equally to free and paid members.

02

Confidential background review

A private background check runs on every application. Results are never shown on your profile — they exist only to keep the platform honest.

03

Human review

A member of our trust and safety team reads every application personally. Nothing is approved by an algorithm alone.

02 — If something goes wrong

Blocking is instant and permanent, and requires no explanation.

Reporting is never ignored, and never automated.

Every report is read by a person on our trust and safety team — not silently closed by a filter. Blocking is instant and permanent, and requires no explanation.

03 — While you're here

Your practical privacy checklist.

Tools built into the platform, not bolted on after.

01

Photo watermarking

Every member photo you view carries a faint mark identifying you specifically — so a leaked or screenshotted copy is traceable back to who took it. Right-click-save and drag are disabled too. No web technique can block a photo of the screen itself; this makes leaking one traceable instead.

02

Black-only visibility

A Black member can opt to be invisible to everyone below Black tier — excluded from Discover, Search, and direct profile links alike. Silver and Gold can still request access; the member decides who gets in.

03

Blurred-photo mode

Your real photo stays hidden until you personally approve someone to see it — even after you've matched.

04

A quiet way out

Blocking is instant, silent, and permanent — the other person is never told you did it, and never sees you again.

05

Self-destructing messages

Black members can set any conversation to auto-delete after a chosen window — for both sides, permanently.

06

Private notes

Gold and Black members can keep a private note about anyone — visible only to them, never to the person it's about.
