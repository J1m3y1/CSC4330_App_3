How admission works

Three checks, before anyone sees you — or you see them.

Membership is never granted on payment alone. Every profile, free or paid, passes through the same review before it becomes visible to anyone else.

01

Identity verification

Government ID is required before a profile can browse at all. No exceptions, no shortcuts — this applies equally to free and paid members.

02

Confidential background review

A private background check runs on every application. Results are never shown on your profile — they exist only to keep the platform honest.

03

Human review

A member of our trust and safety team reads every application personally. Nothing is approved by an algorithm alone.

While you're here

Tools built into the platform, not bolted on after

01

Blurred-photo mode

Your real photo stays hidden until you personally approve someone to see it — even after you've matched.

02

Live check-in for dates

Share real-time location with a trusted contact for the length of a meeting. It expires automatically when the date ends.

03

Duress signal

A quiet way to signal a conversation needs to end, without alerting the other person that anything is wrong.

04

Screenshot protection

Photos shared in conversation are watermarked and protected against screenshotting where technically possible.

05

Self-destructing messages

Set any conversation to auto-delete after a chosen window — for both sides, permanently.

06

Employer blocklist

Manually exclude specific companies from ever seeing your profile — never explained, never questioned.

If something goes wrong

Reporting is never ignored, and never automated.

Every report is read by a person on our trust and safety team — not silently closed by a filter. Blocking is instant and permanent, and requires no explanation.
